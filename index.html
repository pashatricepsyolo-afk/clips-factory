Clips Factory

A personal publishing tool — not a public service.

Clips Factory is a private tool used by a single owner to prepare short gaming videos (YouTube Shorts) and to upload and schedule them on the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API v3.

What it does with Google data

  • Requests one OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl.
  • Uses it only to upload videos as private with a scheduled publish time, to check that the signed-in channel is the intended channel, to read back the uploaded video's metadata, and to correct or delete the owner's own videos.
  • Only the owner signs in. No other users, no accounts are created, nothing is sold or shared.

Privacy policy

Clips Factory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
